\section{Abstract}
This document details the proposal for the design, development, and implementation of ParkLink, a comprehensive digital platform aimed at mitigating parking issues in large urban centers with high vehicular density, using Buenos Aires city (CABA) as the initial scenario. The system centralizes the supply of available spaces through a framework that connects private drivers with owners of underutilized garages, businesses, and traditional parking lots. Through georeferenced mapping, manual booking, authentication, and secure payment processing modules, the project seeks to enable an efficient collaborative economy model. The plan outlines a narrow and scalable scope for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), excluding complex IoT sensor automations in its first stage to mitigate technological risks, and establishes the hybrid methodology (Scrum/Kanban) under which the software development lifecycle will be managed.

\textbf{Keywords:} urban parking, georeferenced mapping, garage booking, collaborative economy, sustainable mobility, space optimization, big cities.
