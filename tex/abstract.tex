\section{Abstract}
This document presents the proposal for the design and development of ParkLink, a digital platform intended to address parking-related issues in densely populated urban environments, using Buenos Aires City (CABA) as the initial case study. The increasing number of private vehicles circulating in large cities has generated significant challenges regarding mobility, traffic congestion, and the efficient use of urban infrastructure. In this context, the difficulty of finding available parking spaces has become a recurring problem for both residents and visitors.

ParkLink aims to provide a technological solution that connects drivers searching for parking spaces with owners of underutilized private garages, commercial parking facilities, and businesses with temporary parking availability. Through a centralized platform featuring georeferenced mapping, user authentication, reservation management, and secure payment processing, the system seeks to optimize the allocation of existing parking resources while reducing unnecessary vehicle circulation.

The proposed project focuses on the development of a scalable Minimum Viable Product (MVP) capable of validating the feasibility and usability of the platform in real-world scenarios. To reduce technological complexity and implementation risks during the initial stages, advanced automation features based on IoT sensors, AI prediction and smart infrastructure will not be included in the first iteration of the system. Instead, the project prioritizes the development of a stable and functional platform centered on accessibility, usability, and operational reliability.

Additionally, this document defines the project scope, the external tools to be evaluated, the validation strategy, and the hybrid agile methodology based on Scrum and Kanban practices that will guide the project. The initiative seeks not only to improve the parking experience for drivers, but also to encourage a more efficient and collaborative use of urban resources, contributing to smarter and more sustainable mobility solutions.
